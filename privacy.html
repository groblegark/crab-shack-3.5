CRAB SHACK — Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 August 2026

CRAB SHACK collects nothing. Not your name, not your email, not an advertising identifier, not a crash report, not a count of how long you played. There is no account to make and nothing to opt out of.

Where your game is kept

Your towns are saved on your own device, in the app's private storage, using the browser storage the app is built on. They are not uploaded anywhere, because there is nowhere to upload them to. Deleting the app deletes them.

The EXPORT button hands a save file to the iOS share sheet so you can put it in Files, AirDrop it, or mail it to yourself. Where it goes from there is entirely your choice — the app never sends it anywhere on its own.

The network

The app makes no network requests. Every file it needs — the game, the art, all twenty-two music tracks — is inside the app itself. It works with the phone in airplane mode, and behaves identically either way.

Third parties

There are none. No analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no attribution, no social logins, no third-party SDKs of any kind.

Children

Because the app collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If a future version ever collects anything, this page will say so before that version ships, and the App Store privacy label will change to match.
